Hi, I'm Avelina

I was born in a small town in Sonora, Mexico, in a family of fields, horses and cows. Now I live far from home in Monterrey.

The guitar has been with me since I was little. My favorite moments are alone with it, improvising and letting out what I feel without overthinking.

I am inspired by Kevin Kaarl, Ed Maverick, Carla Morrison, Sleeping At Last, Natalia Lafourcade and Silvana Estrada. What I am after is a world of my own, with no characters and no alter egos.
